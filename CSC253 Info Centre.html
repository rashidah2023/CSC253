What will I learn?

CSC253 introduces interactive multimedia application development: multimedia concepts, text, graphics, audio, video, animation, project development, authoring tools, hardware and software, file formats, storage and memory management, Internet and mobile multimedia, teamwork, digital marketing, video pitching and commercialisation.
